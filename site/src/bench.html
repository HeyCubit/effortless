Benchmarks

Two benchmarks anyone with the plugin and the claude CLI can run. Neither is a lab result: small prompt sets, one run, one grader. The limits are further down, and they matter.

Does it beat Opus at medium effort?

Not across the board. It matches Opus medium on time, is good enough in 9 of 10 answers, and costs a little less on this mix. It saves much more when most of your prompts are simple.

3. What worked, and what did not

Worked

  • The judge picks an acceptable effort in 93% (Haiku) to 99% (Jev) of prompts.
  • Normal prompts on Sonnet medium were good enough in all 12, at 60 to 66% less cost.
  • Hard prompts on Opus high were better or tied in 5 of 6 against Opus medium.

Did not

  • Haiku on easy prompts missed 2 to 3 of 12: a shell one-liner that breaks in subfolders, a regex with a wrong explanation, a stiff translation, an async explanation that overclaimed.
  • Against Opus medium the whole mix only saves 7%.
  • The cheaper answer is rarely better. The grader is strict and counts small misses as losses.

Fixed along the way

  • The router assumed Opus costs 5x Sonnet. Measured, it is about 2x. The fix is in 1.68.3, which is not published yet. With it, the mod stays on your model more often in a warm chat.

4. Limits

  • Prompts start with no chat history. In a real chat the context is already cached. Moving a prompt to another model means writing that context into the other model's cache, and from a warm Opus chat that costs more than staying put. So the savings above are what you get with a small or cold context. In a long warm chat the mod mostly stays on your model, and saves less. This benchmark does not measure that.
  • Routing here follows the label in the case file, not the live judge. How often the live judge agrees with those labels is benchmark 1.
  • One run per baseline and one grader, from the same model family as the baseline, so it may favour Opus-style answers. Run it twice before quoting a close number.
  • 30 prompts, none of them long agentic sessions with tools.
  • Cost is the CLI's list-price dollars per answer, not your plan's usage.
