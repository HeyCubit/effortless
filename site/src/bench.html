Two tests. One checks how often the judge picks the right effort. The other checks whether the cheaper answer is still good enough next to Opus.

Does it beat Opus at medium effort?

Not across the board. It is as fast, good enough in 9 of 10 answers, and a little cheaper on this mix of prompts. It saves much more when most of your prompts are simple.

What works, and what needs improving

Why it works

Normal prompts keep their quality at a lower price.Sonnet at medium effort was good enough in 12 of 12, at 60 to 66% less cost.
The judge is usually right.Haiku picks an acceptable effort 93% of the time, Jev 99%. A fixed effort manages 44 to 51%.
Hard prompts get more, not less.They go to Opus at high effort, which was better or tied in 5 of 6 against Opus medium.
It is not slower.Median 6.4 s per answer against 7.4 s on Opus medium.

What needs improving

Easy prompts on Haiku miss sometimes.Good enough in 9 to 10 of 12. Misses: a shell one-liner that broke in subfolders, a regex with a wrong explanation, a stiff translation, an async explanation that overclaimed.
Against Opus medium the saving is small.7% on this mix, because hard prompts go to Opus high and cost 26% more there.
The cheaper answer is rarely better.Routed answers beat Opus medium in 3 of 30, two of them hard prompts where routing uses Opus high anyway.

Does the cheaper answer hold up?

The same 30 prompts went two ways: straight to Opus at medium effort, and through effortless, which picks a model and an effort for each one. A grader (Opus, high effort) saw both answers without knowing which was which and said whether the effortless answer would still do the job.

Fixed along the way

The router assumed Opus costs 5 times as much as Sonnet. Measured, it is about 2 times. The fix is in 1.68.3, which is not published yet. With it, the mod stays on your model more often in a warm chat.

Read before quoting

Limits

No chat history

Every prompt starts cold. In a real chat the context is already cached, and moving a prompt to another model means writing that context into the other model's cache. From a warm Opus chat that costs more than staying put, so in a long chat the mod mostly stays on your model and saves less than shown here.

Labels, not the live judge

Benchmark 2 routes by the label in the case file. How often the live judge agrees with those labels is benchmark 1.

One run, one grader

One run per baseline, graded by a model from the same family as the baseline, so it may favour Opus-style answers. Run it twice before quoting a close number.

Small and short

30 prompts, none of them long agentic sessions with tools. Cost is list price per answer, not your plan's usage.
